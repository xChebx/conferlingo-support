Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Overview

ConferLingo does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service, or translation server. The app processes your content on your device and sends required requests directly to OpenAI using the API key you provide.

Audio and text

When you start a translation, microphone audio and, on supported Macs, system audio are sent to OpenAI for speech recognition, translation, and optional speech generation. Transcribed and translated text is also sent to OpenAI when required to complete those features. OpenAI handles this data under the terms and privacy choices applying to your OpenAI API account.

Local data

Translation history, drafts, preferences, usage estimates, and custom glossary entries are stored locally on your device. Your OpenAI API key is stored in Apple Keychain. ConferLingo does not receive this local data. Removing the app may remove locally stored content; exported Markdown files remain wherever you saved them.

System audio and screen

On recent macOS versions ConferLingo uses system audio capture without requesting screen image content. On older compatible versions, macOS may categorize the permission under Screen & System Audio Recording. ConferLingo does not record, transmit, or store screenshots or screen video.

Tracking and advertising

ConferLingo does not track users across apps or websites, display advertising, sell personal information, or use third-party advertising identifiers.

Your choices

You control when recording begins and ends. You can clear app history, remove custom terms, delete the API key, revoke microphone or system-audio permission in system settings, and delete exported files.

Consent and lawful recording

You are responsible for notifying participants and obtaining consent to record or process conversations where required by applicable law or organizational policy.
